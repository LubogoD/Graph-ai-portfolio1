About Me

Academic profile, research interests, and institutional background

Bio & Research Focus

Dr. David Lubogo is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Community Health and Behavioural Sciences at Makerere University School of Public Health (MakSPH) in Kampala, Uganda. His work spans public health research, epidemiological studies, nutrition, non-communicable diseases/metabolic syndrome, HIV/AIDS, disaster risk management, and health inequality monitoring.

As a Principal Investigator and Co-Investigator on projects funded by the Government of Uganda / Makerere University Research and Innovation Fund (MakRIF), the World Food Programme, and Save the Children, his recent research focuses on metabolic syndrome, nutritional assessment, and community-based health interventions.

About the Course

Generative AI for Work & Research: No-Code AI Tools for Responsible Task Automation

Program Overview

Offered by The GRAPH Courses (based at the University of Geneva), this 8-week live cohort bootcamp teaches researchers and professionals how to responsibly leverage large language models (LLMs) and no-code AI tools for task automation, text processing, data analysis, and scientific communication.

7 Modules Practical Core Curriculum
No-Code AI Responsible Automation

Course Curriculum

Module 1

Documents, Presentations & Websites

Prompting LLMs, generating DOCX, PPTX, and HTML outputs, building research websites, and using AI detectors responsibly.

Module 2

AI-Automated Data Analysis

Data agents, spreadsheet manipulation, quick dashboarding, and spotting statistical hallucinations in AI outputs.

Module 3

GenAI for Text Processing & Research

Extracting structured data from unstructured text, AI-powered literature review tools, and qualitative data analysis.

Module 4

Custom GPTs and Chatbots

Building chatbots with custom knowledge bases, understanding RAG, context windows, and internal-facing custom GPTs.

Module 5

AI for Task Automation

LLM-powered automations, triggered actions, browser control agents, and research digests.

Module 6

GenAI for Communication & Outreach

Image generation models, visual abstracts for research papers, scientific diagrams, and AI-assisted flyers.

Module 7

Capstone Project & Showcase

Collaborating to build and refine a final AI solution, addressing technical challenges, and presenting the final project.
